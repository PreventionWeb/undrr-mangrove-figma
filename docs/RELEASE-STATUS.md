## Remaining gates for affected releases

- Extracted-plugin execution in an unpublished rehearsal file, with source revisions and full operation reports recorded.
- Source/native visual comparison, fonts/glyphs, genuine owning edits and reflow, bindings/styles, relevant modes, repeat-build identity and geometry.
- Recovery verification for the tiny layout drift seen during ordinary Button rebuilds. No production rounding fix is justified by current evidence.
- Deliberately changed text/effect-style publication and restoration.
- Composite publication and genuine consumer uptake/restoration, preserving links, overrides, modes and recorded geometry.
- Human novice native operation and one real designer/developer handoff accepted against Storybook output.

Routine changes apply the gates relevant to the affected assets. Broad landing/news/search assembly is preserved experimental work; adopting it requires its own focused proposal and acceptance. Token publication does not satisfy component/style/page gates.

No acceptance of those scopes is claimed; detailed negative evidence is preserved in the evidence guide.
